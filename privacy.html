Carrom Crew — Privacy Policy

Updated 25 September 2026 for Carrom Crew 1.1.0 · applies to Carrom Crew 1.0.0 and later

Summary

We do not sell your data for money, and the game has no analytics of its own. Starting with version 1.1.0, Carrom Crew shows ads delivered through Unity LevelPlay, which fills them from our ad partners Unity Ads and Google AdMob. These partners process some device data, such as your advertising identifier, to show and measure ads. They are asked to personalise ads only if you allow it (and, on iPhone and iPad, only if you also allow tracking). See "Advertising" and "Your ad choices". Your game progress is stored on your device. Online matches, friends and leaderboards send some data to our server (see "Online matches" and "Friends and leaderboards"). Sign in with Apple is optional on iPhone and iPad; if you use it, a copy of your progress is kept on our server so it loads on your other devices (see "Optional Sign in with Apple and cloud save"). You can delete your account and its data in the app at any time (see "Deleting your account").

What the app stores on your device

  • Your progress: level, XP, trophies, owned items, Crew Pass progress, settings, missions and achievements, and the results of your last 30 matches.
  • A random guest identifier (g-…) created on first launch. It contains no personal information.
  • The display name you choose (defaults to "Player").
  • From version 1.1.0: your answer to the "Personalised ads" question, whether you own Remove Ads, and the counters that keep ads spaced out (how many matches you have finished, when the last ad between matches was shown and how many reward videos you have watched today).

This lives in the app's private storage. To erase it, use Settings → Privacy & data → Reset all data or uninstall the app.

Optional Sign in with Apple and cloud save

On iPhone and iPad you can use Sign in with Apple from the sign-in page or from Settings → Account & name. Signing in is never required: guests can play everything. We never see or store your password. From the sign-in we keep: the sign-in provider (Apple), the account id Apple gives us for Carrom Crew, the e-mail address you choose to share (Apple lets you hide it; we then receive Apple's private relay address instead) and the random guest identifier of the device you signed in on, which links the account to your online profile. We use these only to recognise your account. While you are signed in, the app uploads a copy of your game progress (display name, level, XP, trophies, owned items, Crew Pass, missions and achievements, recent match results and settings) to our server and loads it when you sign in on another device. Signing out keeps your progress on the device and stops syncing. We never pass your account, e-mail address or player id to the ad partners.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Account & name → Delete account. This immediately removes from our server the sign-in link, your cloud save, your e-mail address, your online profile, rating, friends, blocks, online match results and leaderboard entries. Reports you made, and reports other players made about you, are kept for moderation. Your progress on the device stays until you use Reset all data or uninstall the app. You can also stop using Sign in with Apple for Carrom Crew on your iPhone or iPad under Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple. Data held by the ad partners is not stored by us; see "Your ad choices" for how to ask them.

Playing against the computer

Matches against computer opponents, trick-shot puzzles and the lessons run entirely on your device; nothing you do in them leaves your device. Ads are never shown during a match or during the lessons.

Online matches

If you play online (a quick or ranked match, or a room with a friend), the app connects to the Carrom Crew match server to pair you with another player. During the match the server receives your display name, your chosen character, a temporary session token and your moves (striker position, aim and the resulting board), and relays them to your opponent, who sees your display name and character. Your moves are kept in memory only while the match is running and are not stored. The result of a ranked match (winner, loser, how the match ended, rating change and time) is stored with your online profile so ratings and leaderboards work; quick matches and friend rooms are not recorded. The server does not store your IP address beyond the open connection. There is no chat — only the built-in emote stickers can be sent.

Friends and leaderboards

When the app is online it opens a connection to the Carrom Crew server for friends and leaderboards. The server stores a pseudonymous player id (a keyed hash of the random guest id created on your device — not a device identifier, your name or your e-mail), your display name, your chosen character, your rating and online wins and losses, your trick-shot star totals, your friend code and your friends list. Other players can see your display name, character, rating and star totals on leaderboards, and your friends can see whether you are online. Friend invites carry your name and a private room code and expire after a minute. The rating of online matches is worked out by the server from the match result. This profile is kept while you play. Signed-in players remove it with Delete account (see above); players who are not signed in can ask for deletion by e-mail to the contact address below, including the friend code shown on the Friends screen.

Reporting and blocking

You can report another player's name or behaviour and block players from the Leaderboards, Friends and online result screens. A report stores your pseudonymous player id, the reported player's display name (and their player id if they have an online profile), the reason, the match id if any and the time, so we can review it. Blocking stores the pair of player ids. Names reported by several players are hidden, and offensive names are refused.

Advertising

Version 1.0.0 contains the Unity Ads software but never switches it on. Starting with version 1.1.0, Carrom Crew shows two kinds of ads:

  • Rewarded videos — only when you tap to watch one for the reward named on the button (for example bonus XP in the Shop). Closing one early simply gives no reward.
  • Short ads between matches — shown on the way out of a finished match, at most after every second match and at least two minutes apart. They are never shown during a match, during the lessons, after your first or second finished match, or once you own Remove Ads.

The ads are delivered by Unity LevelPlay, the ad mediation service of Unity Technologies (formerly ironSource). For each ad, LevelPlay picks an ad from one of our ad partners: Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) or Google AdMob (Google). Their software in the app collects the data below and sends it to them; we do not receive it. They use it under their own privacy policies:

  • Advertising identifier and other device identifiers: on Android, the Google advertising ID (unless you have deleted it in Android settings) and the app set ID; on iPhone and iPad, Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, and the identifier for vendor (IDFV).
  • Device information: device model and maker, operating system and version, language, time zone, screen size, network type and mobile carrier, and the app's name and version.
  • Ad interactions: which ads were requested, shown, watched to the end, skipped or tapped, and whether an advertised app was later installed.
  • Approximate location: estimated from your IP address (roughly country or city). The game never uses GPS and never asks for location permission.
  • Diagnostic data about the ad software, such as load times, errors and crashes.

The ad partners use this data to deliver ads, to choose which ads to show, to limit how often you see the same ad, to measure how ads perform (which is how we are paid), and to detect fraud and invalid traffic. If you have not allowed personalised ads, we tell them so and they are asked to show only non-personalised ads, chosen from context such as the game and your approximate location rather than from a profile of you; they still process the data above to deliver, limit, measure and protect the ads. Their policies: Unity (Unity LevelPlay, ironSource and Unity Ads), Google and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Ads need an internet connection. If an ad cannot be loaded, you simply keep playing. We do not choose the individual ads that the partners show; if you see an ad that seems inappropriate, please tell us at the address below.

Your ad choices

  • Personalised ads. Once, at first launch after the Start screen, the game asks whether you allow personalised ads: Allow or No thanks. The game plays the same whatever you answer, you see the same number of ads, and neither answer earns a reward. You can change your answer at any time with the Personalised ads switch in Settings; it applies to the ads loaded after the change.
  • Tracking on iPhone and iPad. After that question, iOS asks once whether Carrom Crew may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the ad partners cannot use Apple's advertising identifier. You can change it at any time in the iPhone or iPad Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID. You can delete or reset it in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Remove Ads. A one-time purchase in the Shop that turns off the ads between matches for good. Rewarded videos stay available, but they only ever play when you choose to watch one. It is restored with Settings → Restore purchases and is part of your cloud save when you are signed in with Apple.
  • Data the ad partners hold. We cannot see or delete it. To access or delete it, contact Unity or Google through the policies linked above; they recognise you by your advertising identifier. Some US state laws call giving ad partners your advertising identifier for personalised ads a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information: turning off Personalised ads tells the partners that you do not agree to personalised advertising.

Purchases

Carrom Crew sells optional cosmetic items (strikers, boards and effects), character packs, the Everything Bundle, the Crew Pass and, from version 1.1.0, Remove Ads through the Apple App Store and Google Play billing. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google Play; the app only receives a confirmation that the purchase succeeded, and we never see your payment details. Purchases stay with your App Store or Google Play account and can be restored with Settings → Restore purchases.

Children

Carrom Crew is not directed to children. Its content is rated for all ages (App Store 4+, Everyone / PEGI 3), but the game, its online features and its ads are intended for players aged 13 and older, and we tell the ad partners that the game is not child-directed. The game never asks for a real name, age, address, phone number or location, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information (for example an e-mail address through Sign in with Apple), contact us and we will delete it.

Permissions

The app uses network access (for online play, friends and leaderboards, the optional cloud save, ads and store purchases), the store's billing service for purchases, and the device's vibration for haptic feedback (it can be turned off in Settings). From version 1.1.0 the Android version declares the advertising ID permission used by the ad partners, and on iPhone and iPad the game asks once for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). It does not access the camera, microphone, contacts, device location or photo library.

Changes to this policy

When the app changes how it handles data, we update this page before that version is released; the date at the top shows the latest change.
