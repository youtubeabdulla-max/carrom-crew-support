Who we are

Carrom Crew is made and published by Abdulla Alhashmi, an independent game developer based in the United Arab Emirates ("we", "us"). We decide how the data described in this policy is used and are responsible for it. You can reach us at youtubeabdulla@gmail.com with any question about this policy, to ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted.

Summary

  • We do not sell your data for money, and the game has no analytics software of its own. (Our server keeps totals about online matches, with no player id; see Computer players in Quick matches.)
  • Our server knows you by your player id: a scrambled code made from the random guest identifier on your device, which can't be turned back into it. The law calls this a "pseudonymous" id. It is not your name, your e-mail or an identifier of your device.
  • Carrom Crew shows ads delivered through Unity LevelPlay, which picks each ad from our ad partners Unity Ads, ironSource Ads and Google AdMob. These partners process some device data, such as your advertising identifier, to show and measure ads. The Unity Ads software may also record the in-app purchases made on your device. We tell them they may personalise ads only if you say yes. On iPhone and iPad, they can use Apple's advertising identifier only if you allow tracking. See Advertising and Your ad choices.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device. While you play as a guest, the game also keeps a copy of your guest identifier, so that a reinstall keeps your online profile (see Reinstalling, backups and your guest identifier).
  • While the game is online it keeps a small online profile on our server for friends, leaderboards and online play, which includes your trick-shot star totals and the optional gender and flag shown next to your name (see Friends and leaderboards, Gender and flag and Online matches).
  • When few players are online, a Quick match may be against a computer player instead of a long wait. Computer players never change ratings or the leaderboards (see Computer players in Quick matches).
  • Sign in with Apple is optional on iPhone and iPad. If you use it, a copy of your progress is kept on our server, so it loads on your other devices (see Optional Sign in with Apple and cloud save).
  • If you chat with your friends, our server keeps your messages for up to 30 days (see Chat with friends). The medals you earn are shown with your online profile (see Medals).
  • Your game has a support code, so that our team can find you when you ask for help and send you gifts. A second server of ours, our support service, keeps it. It holds a one-way code made from your guest identifier, never the identifier, your name or your e-mail address (see Your support code and gifts from our team).
  • You can delete your online data in the app at any time (see Deleting your data).

What the app stores on your device

  • Your progress: level, XP, trophies, owned items, trick-shot stars, cup, Weekend League and weekly ladder progress, streaks and best times, settings, missions and achievements, and the results of your last 30 matches.
  • The names of the last 20 computer players you met in Quick matches, so that you do not meet the same one again soon (see Computer players in Quick matches).
  • A random guest identifier (g-…) created on first launch. It contains no personal information. While you play as a guest, the app also keeps a copy of it outside the app's storage, so that it survives a reinstall (see Reinstalling, backups and your guest identifier).
  • The display name you choose (defaults to "Player").
  • Your gender setting (Male, Female or Hidden) and your flag setting (a country or region, or No flag).
  • If you sign in with Apple: the sign-in provider, the account id Apple gives Carrom Crew, the e-mail address you chose to share and a session key that keeps this device signed in. The game uses the account id only to check with Apple that you still use Sign in with Apple for Carrom Crew. To show them in Settings → Account & name, the app also keeps the date your account was created, the time of its last cloud save and your Player ID. It also keeps an account key (a random-looking code, not your account id), so it knows which account this device's progress belongs to. The account key stays after you sign out and is removed by Reset all data.
  • Your answer to the "Personalised ads" question, whether you own Remove Ads, and the counters that pace the ads: how many matches you have finished, when the last ad between matches was shown and how many reward videos you have watched today.
  • Your age group from the one-time age question (under 13, or 13 and older). For a player under 13, the device also keeps the year they turn 14, so that the game can move them to 13 and older by itself in that year. Their year of birth can be worked out from it. We don't keep the year you type (see Your age).
  • Which version of the Terms of Service you accepted on the game's first screen, and when (the date and time, in UTC).
  • Your support code, which our server sends the game, and the ids of the gifts from our team that the game has applied, so that a gift is never given twice (see Your support code and gifts from our team).
  • A few small notes kept next to the save: the Fine aim switch and how many Fine aim shots you have fired (up to three, for its hint); the week number and your trick-shot star total at the start of that week (to count this week's stars); and a hidden switch used only to test the ads, which stays empty unless a tester turns it on.
  • Only if your save ever failed to load: a full copy of that save, kept so that it could be recovered. It can hold anything a save holds, including, if you were signed in at the time, the e-mail address and the session key.
  • The store software's own record of each purchase confirmed on this device (named by a scrambled form of the store's transaction id), so that a purchase is never added twice. The ad partners' software and, on Android, Google's consent form for ads also keep their own data on the device, such as your ad consent choices.
  • The Unity engine, which the game is built with, may keep its own small records next to the save, such as a random id and a count of sessions.

This lives in the app's private storage. To erase it, use Settings → Privacy & data → Reset all data or uninstall the app.

  • Of your game's save, Reset all data keeps only your age group (and, for a player under 13, the year they turn 14), so the age question is not asked again.
  • Reset all data also erases the names of computer players you met, the small notes kept next to the save and the copy of a save that failed to load. It leaves the store's purchase records and the ad partners' and Google's data described above. Uninstalling the app removes those from the device.
  • Neither Reset all data nor uninstalling deletes anything on our game server or our support service (see Deleting your data).

Uninstalling does not erase everything:

  • The kept copy of your guest identifier stays.
  • On Android, if backup is on, Android's backup keeps the game's save and gives it back when you install the game again.
  • Backups of your iPhone or iPad keep the save too.

The next section explains these.

Reinstalling, backups and your guest identifier

So that you keep the same online profile (your Player ID, friends and rating) when you reinstall the game, the app keeps a copy of your random guest identifier outside the app's storage. It keeps one only while you play as a guest. While you are signed in it keeps none: your account comes back when you sign in again.

  • On iPhone and iPad: the copy is in the device's keychain, for this device only. It is not synced to iCloud Keychain and never moves to another device. iOS currently keeps it when you delete the app (Apple does not promise this), so a reinstall on the same device continues with the same online profile. Your progress is not kept this way: after a reinstall it starts again (sign in to load a cloud save), and purchases come back with Settings → Restore purchases. The copy never leaves the device by itself. The identifier in it reaches our server only in the messages described in this policy: when the game connects, when you sign in and when you delete your online data.
  • On Android: Android's own backup can include two files of the game: the file with the kept guest identifier, and the file in which the game keeps its save and the small notes described above. It includes them if backup is on in your phone's settings, and when you copy your apps and data to a new phone.
    • So the backup holds your progress and settings, your age group (and, under 13, the year you turn 14), your guest identifier, your support code, the ids of the gifts from our team already applied, the names of computer players you met, the Fine aim and weekly star notes and, if a save ever failed to load, the copy of it. It may also hold the Unity engine's small records described above. You can't sign in on Android, so it holds no e-mail address or sign-in key.
    • Google keeps that backup in your Google Account under its own terms; we never receive it. It stays there after you uninstall the game.
    • A reinstall, or a new phone set up from that backup or by a transfer from your old phone, continues where you left off.
    • The store's purchase records, the ad partners' data and Google's ad consent choices are not in this backup.
  • Backups of your iPhone or iPad: like the data of other apps, the game's data on the device is part of the backups you make of your iPhone or iPad to iCloud or to a computer. That includes its save, the small notes, the store's purchase records and, while you are signed in, the e-mail address and session key. It may also include the data the ad partners' software keeps on the device. A device set up from such a backup gets it back, signed in or not as it was. You and Apple keep those backups; we never receive them. The keychain copy of the guest identifier is for this device only and does not move to another device this way.

Delete online data, Delete account, signing out and Reset all data each give the device a new guest identifier, and the kept copy is replaced with it at once. Signing in removes the copy. On Android the backup takes the change at its next run: Android backs up at most about once a day, when the phone is idle. Until then, a reinstall from the older backup brings back the old save and identifier.

If you reinstall from an older backup, the restored game goes online with the old identifier. As at every start, it sends the name, character, gender, flag and medals kept in that save. What happens then:

  • Within one year of a deletion (with Delete online data, Delete account, when a device's guest profile is merged into your account as you sign in on it, or by us at your request): our server recognises the identifier. It stores nothing of what was sent and tells the game, which takes a new guest identifier and stays offline, as right after the deletion. Your deleted data and Player ID do not come back.
  • More than a year after the deletion: the old identifier starts a new online profile. It gets a new Player ID and the name, character, gender, flag, medals and trick-shot stars kept in that save. On our support service it gets a new, empty record with the same support code as before. Use Delete online data to remove both.

Optional Sign in with Apple and cloud save

On iPhone and iPad you can use Sign in with Apple from the sign-in page or from Settings → Account & name. It is not offered to players under 13 (see Children). Signing in is never required: guests can play everything. We never see or store your password. The game asks Apple only for your e-mail address, never your name.

From the sign-in we keep:

  • the sign-in provider (Apple) and the account id Apple gives us for Carrom Crew, which we use to recognise your account;
  • the e-mail address you choose to share. Apple lets you hide it; we then receive Apple's private relay address instead. We use the address only to show you which account you signed in with and to confirm it is you if you write to us. We never send you marketing e-mail.
  • the random guest identifier of the device you signed in on, which links the account to your online profile;
  • a session key that keeps that device signed in, stored only as a scrambled code that can't be turned back into the key;
  • the token Apple gives our server for your sign-in (Apple calls it a "refresh token"). We use it only to ask Apple to revoke Carrom Crew's Sign in with Apple access: when you delete your account, or when a new account is not created (see below);
  • when your account was created and when its cloud save was last updated. The app shows both in Settings → Account & name.

When a sign-in goes through, our server also tells our support service. It sends one-way codes made from the account id and from the e-mail address you share, never the id or the address itself, and the support service adds a secret to each before it keeps it. This lets our team find your record when you write to us from that address (see Your support code and gifts from our team). If another record there already holds that account, nothing moves by itself: our team looks at it.

Before an account is created

When you sign in, the app first shows you whether an account already exists for that sign-in. Until you tap Create account (or choose which progress to keep), our server holds the sign-in result only in its memory, for up to 10 minutes, and creates nothing. The sign-in result is the account id, the e-mail address and a one-time code from Apple or the token it was exchanged for.

  • If you tap Not now, or don't answer within the 10 minutes, the sign-in result is dropped.
  • For a new account, our server then asks Apple to revoke the token, which removes Carrom Crew from your Apple Account again. Until Apple confirms, the token and a scrambled code made from your Apple user id are kept for that one purpose, for up to 7 days.

Signing in on a device with a guest profile

If you sign in on a device to an account that already exists, that device's guest profile is merged into your account. Its friends, friend requests, blocks, chats and mutes move to your account's profile. The rest of it (name, character, gender, flag, friend code, rating and results) is deleted from our server. The app shows you this before you confirm. Our server keeps the id of that merged guest profile for one year, as it does for every deleted profile, and nothing else of it (see Reinstalling, backups and your guest identifier).

The merge does not touch that guest profile's record on our support service, so that a gift waiting there is not lost. That record keeps its own support code. It stays there until our team joins it to your account's record, or deletes it when you ask us to (see What a deletion removes, and what stays).

Cloud save

While you are signed in, the app uploads a copy of your game progress to our server and loads it when you sign in on another device:

  • your display name, gender and flag settings, level, XP, trophies, owned items and characters, and purchases such as Remove Ads;
  • missions, achievements, trick-shot stars, cup, Weekend League and weekly ladder progress, streaks and best times;
  • your medals and the medal shown next to your name, your equipped items and character, the lessons you have done and your game statistics (such as shots played and your login streak);
  • your 20 most recent match results (including matches against the computer: result, opponent's name, a match id, discs pocketed, place, XP and time);
  • your 20 latest inbox messages, the ids of the gifts from our team already applied, the ad counters and which version of the Terms of Service you accepted.

The copy leaves out the sign-in details, the guest identifier, your support code, the app's settings (such as sound, language, your age group and your ad choice) and a few notes that only matter on that device. Items and purchases already in your cloud save stay in it, even if an older version of the game uploads a copy without them.

  • Signing out keeps your progress on the device and stops syncing. The app first sends a last copy of your progress.
  • Only one device at a time stays signed in to an account. The exception: an iPhone or iPad set up from a backup of a signed-in device is also signed in, until either device signs out or signs in again.
  • We never pass your account, e-mail address or player id to the ad partners.

Deleting your data

If you signed in with Apple

Use Settings → Account & name → Delete account at any time; the game asks you to confirm once with Apple.

  • This immediately deletes from our server the sign-in link, your cloud save, your e-mail address, your online profile (including your gender and flag), rating, friends and friend requests (declined ones too), blocks, ranked match results and leaderboard entries, and your medals, chat messages and mutes, and the record of which version of the Terms of Service you accepted and when. Only a hidden copy of your chat messages is kept, for 14 days (see below).
  • Our server also asks Apple to revoke Carrom Crew's Sign in with Apple access, which removes Carrom Crew from your Apple Account, and deletes the Apple refresh token with your account. If Apple can't complete that right away, our server keeps that token for this one purpose and tries again for up to 7 days. It keeps a scrambled code made from your Apple user id with it, so that the retry is dropped if you sign in with the same Apple ID again.
  • If Carrom Crew still appears in your Apple Account afterwards, you can remove it yourself: open the iPhone or iPad Settings app, tap your name, then Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple, choose Carrom Crew and stop using Sign in with Apple for it.
  • It also clears your record on our support service (see What a deletion removes, and what stays).
  • After the deletion, the app keeps your progress on the device as a guest. It stays offline until you open Friends or Leaderboards or play online; nothing is sent to our server before that.

If you play as a guest

Use Settings → Privacy & data → Delete online data at any time.

  • This immediately deletes from our server your online profile (display name, character, gender, flag, friend code, rating and trick-shot star totals), your friends and friend requests (declined ones too), blocks, ranked match results and leaderboard entries, and your medals, chat messages and mutes, and the record of which version of the Terms of Service you accepted and when. Only a hidden copy of your chat messages is kept, for 14 days (see below).
  • It also clears your record on our support service (see What a deletion removes, and what stays).
  • Your progress on the device stays.
  • The game then stays offline until you go online on purpose (by opening Friends or Leaderboards, playing online or signing in). That starts a new profile with a new friend code.

By e-mail

You can also e-mail youtubeabdulla@gmail.com with the friend code shown on the Friends screen. The app also shows it as your Player ID in Settings → Account & name. So that nobody can delete someone else's data, we first confirm it is you:

  • For an account, write from the e-mail address shown in Settings → Account & name.
  • If Apple hid your address there or none is shown, or for a guest profile, we instead ask you to change your in-game name to a word we send you.

We then delete the same data as Delete account or Delete online data does, within one month (usually much sooner), and confirm by e-mail.

A parent can ask the same way for a child's data.

On the web

The page Delete your Carrom Crew account and data explains these ways to delete your data. It works without the app.

On your device

Settings → Privacy & data → Reset all data, or uninstalling the app, deletes nothing on our game server or on our support service.

  • Your online profile and your record on our support service stay, and so do your account and cloud save if you are signed in.
  • After Reset all data, or a reinstall that does not bring back your guest identifier, the app can no longer reach that online profile or that record. Neither server deletes unused profiles or records by itself. We can delete them on request only if we can confirm they were yours. So use Delete online data or Delete account first.
  • Reset all data also replaces the kept copy of the guest identifier. Uninstalling leaves that copy. On Android, if backup is on, Android's backup also keeps the save and gives it back on a reinstall, until Reset all data has run and Android's next backup has replaced it (see Reinstalling, backups and your guest identifier).

What a deletion removes, and what stays

  • Deleting your online data or your account removes your chat messages from your friends' screens at once. We keep a hidden copy for 14 days so that abuse can still be reported, then delete it.
  • It also deletes which messages you have read, the counts of your refused messages and of friends who never wrote back, and any chat pause, hold or ban on your profile.
  • Reports you made, and reports other players made about you (with the copies of reported messages), are kept for moderation, and so is our log of moderation actions (see How long data is kept).
  • In a separate list, our server keeps only the player id of the deleted profile and the date, for one year, so that the profile stays deleted (see Reinstalling, backups and your guest identifier).
  • It also clears your record on our support service (see Your support code and gifts from our team), and any older record our team has joined to it. Any gift still waiting for you is cancelled. Gone from the record:
    • the one-way codes of your guest identifier and, if you signed in, of your account id and e-mail address;
    • your support code;
    • your platform and app version, the dates and how many times the game connected;
    • the reason and order number on your gifts;
    • the details on the activity trail.
  • Our game server tells the support service straight after the deletion. If the support service can't be reached, our game server keeps the one-way code of your guest identifier in a queue and keeps trying until it answers.
  • What stays on our support service holds nothing that identifies you, and has no end date: an empty record (an internal number, the game and the time of the deletion), each gift's item, amount, state and dates, without who or why, and the activity trail's event names and times.
  • Deleted data can stay in backups for a while: up to 5 days for our game server and up to about 30 days for our support service (see How long data is kept). A deletion also does not reach a full copy of the support service's data that someone on our team downloaded before it.
  • A deletion clears only the record that belongs to your current guest identifier or account. A guest profile merged into your account at a sign-in has its own record, unless our team has joined the two. To have it deleted, e-mail us, with its support code if you still have it. The same goes for the record of an identifier you no longer use (for example after Reset all data).
  • Data held by the ad partners is not stored by us; see Your ad choices for how to ask them.

Playing against the computer

Matches against computer opponents, trick-shot puzzles and the lessons are played on your device, and the moves you make in them are never sent anywhere. (A computer player you meet in a Quick match is different: that match is played online; see Computer players in Quick matches.)

Some results do leave the device:

  • your trick-shot star totals go to our server for the leaderboards (see Friends and leaderboards);
  • a copy of your progress and recent match results is uploaded if you are signed in (see Optional Sign in with Apple and cloud save);
  • when an ad is shown after a match, the ad partners receive the data described in Advertising.

Ads are never shown during a match or during the lessons.

Online matches

If you play online (a quick or ranked match, or a room with a friend), the app connects to the Carrom Crew match server to pair you with another player. When few players are online, a Quick match may instead be against a computer player (see Computer players in Quick matches).

  • What the server receives: during the match, your display name, your chosen character, your gender and flag (unless set to Hidden or No flag), a temporary session token and your moves (striker position, aim and the resulting board). It relays them to your opponent, who sees your display name, character, gender icon and flag.
  • Your opponent: Your opponent's game also receives the disc set you play with and, once the game has linked the match to your online profile, your pseudonymous player id (see Friends and leaderboards), so that your opponent can send you a friend request for 24 hours after the match. Your opponent also sees the medal shown next to your name, if you have one (see "Medals").
  • What is stored: Your moves are kept in memory only while the match is running and are not stored. The result of a ranked match (winner, loser, how the match ended, rating change and time) is stored with your online profile, so that ratings and leaderboards work; the results of quick matches and friend rooms are not stored.
  • What the server keeps in its memory: For every online match (quick, ranked or a friend room) the server keeps the pair of the two players' ids in its memory for 24 hours, not stored, only so that the two players can send each other a friend request. (For a ranked match, the stored result also serves this purpose.) It also keeps the two players' match details (names, characters, flags, gender icons, disc sets, medals and player ids) in its memory for an hour after the match, so that either player can still report the other.
  • Your IP address: The server does not store your IP address beyond the open connection. To limit requests, it may count them per address in its memory, under a scrambled code made from the address with a key that changes every time the server starts. This is never written to disk or to the logs. Fly.io, which runs our server, sees your IP address as part of carrying the connection (see Who else handles data).
  • Messages: In a match you can only send the built-in stickers and phrases. Chat with friends is described below.

Computer players in Quick matches

When few players are online, a Quick match may be against a computer player instead of a long wait. Computer players never change ratings or the leaderboards.

  • When: only in a Quick match, after a short wait with nobody else looking for the same kind of match. Ranked matches and rooms with a friend never use computer players.
  • Its name and look: our server sets up the match like any online match. It picks the computer player's name, character, disc set, gender icon, flag and medal at random for that one match, from fixed lists that come with our server. They are not anyone's profile, and they are not stored. Other players' profiles are used only to make sure the name is not one a player uses.
  • How it plays: your own device plays the computer player's moves, with the same rules and physics as vs Computer, and they go through our server like the moves of any online match. Your own moves go only to our server and back to your device.
  • What the app sends for it: when you start looking for a Quick match, the app also sends our server your trophy count and the names of the last 20 computer players you met (kept on your device, so that you do not meet the same one again soon). If you have played ranked matches, it also sends your ranked rating and how many ranked matches you have played, which our server already holds with your online profile. What the app sends for this is not stored.
  • Its strength: our server uses what the app sends only to set up a Quick match against a computer player. It sets the computer player's strength from your ranked rating if you have played ranked matches, otherwise from your trophy count, aiming for an even match. We can also set the difficulty for everyone from our server. It never depends on what you buy or on the ads you watch.
  • No player id: a computer player has no player id, so no friend request can be sent either way and no pair of player ids is kept. It sends only the built-in stickers and never chats. The match is not rated and never appears on a leaderboard. Our server keeps no record of it apart from the daily totals described below, the server log and any report you make about it (see How long data is kept).
  • On your device it counts like any online match: XP, trophies, weekly ladder points, medals and streaks, and a line in your recent match results (part of your cloud save if you are signed in).

To tune the wait and the difficulty, our server keeps daily totals about the Quick queue and these matches (for example how many were played, how long players waited, wins by mode and level, and how many were reported). For online matches between people, it also writes to its log, once an hour, totals of how long players took before a shot and how many stickers were sent. Those hourly totals are not stored anywhere else. None of these totals contains a player id, name or match id.

Friends and leaderboards

On iPhone and iPad, the game first connects to the Carrom Crew server when the first-launch screens reach the optional sign-in page (players under 13 don't see that page). Otherwise, it first connects a few seconds after you finish the first-launch screens. After that it connects by itself whenever the game runs with an internet connection, even if you never play online. (After you use Delete online data, it waits until you go online on purpose; see Deleting your data.)

The server then keeps an online profile for your device, stored under your player id. It holds:

  • your display name, your chosen character, your gender and flag (see Gender and flag), your friend code and your friends list;
  • when the profile was created and last seen;
  • your ranked rating and your ranked wins and losses;
  • your trick-shot star totals (all-time and this week), which the game sends by itself whenever it connects, after each match and when you open the leaderboards;
  • how many online matches against other players you have finished. Typed chat opens only for a profile that is at least a day old and has finished one, or is signed in.
  • your medals (see "Medals"), your age group and chat settings (see Chat with friends), and the state of any hidden name, chat pause, hold or ban (see Reporting and blocking).
  • which version of the Terms of Service you accepted in the game, and when: the time your device gave when you tapped I agree, and the time our server received it (the game sends it once it is online).

Other players can see your display name, character, gender icon, flag, rating and star totals on the leaderboards, including the public Trick shots and This week boards. Your friends can see whether you are online or in a match.

  • Friend requests: other players can send you a friend request with your friend code, from your row while you are on a leaderboard, or for 24 hours after an online match with you; you choose whether to accept it. If you decline a request, the server keeps the pair of player ids for 7 days, so that the same player can't ask again right away.
  • Invites: Friend invites carry your name, gender icon, flag and a private room code; they also show your character. The server holds the invite itself only in its memory, until your friend answers it or it is cleared as out of date. The invite disappears from your friend's screen after a minute. Each invite is also stored as a line in your chat with that friend (with the room code), like any chat message (see Chat with friends).

Chat with friends

You can chat only with players you have accepted as friends. Quick messages and stickers are fixed texts and pictures. Typed messages are up to 120 characters, and only players aged 13 and older who have accepted the Chat rules can send or receive them.

  • When typing opens: only for a profile that is at least a day old and has finished an online match against another player, or is signed in. With a friend you added after a match or from a leaderboard, typing opens 24 hours after you became friends.
  • What is stored: our server stores each message for up to 30 days and at most the last 100 messages with each friend, so that it arrives when your friend is offline. For each message it keeps who sent it, to which friend, the quick-message or sticker name, the typed text or the room of an invite, and the time. It also keeps which messages you have read, which friends you muted, whether you accepted the Chat rules and your typing setting, with your online profile.
  • Automatic checks: typed messages are checked automatically and refused:
    • if they contain bad words, links, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, other contact details, requests for private information or scams;
    • if the same message is sent again and again in a short time;
    • if they use characters the chat does not allow, such as emoji;
    • if you send too many too fast.
    Typed messages are never written to the server's logs.
  • Counts: We count refused messages per player and day (the number only, not the text) to spot abuse; counts are kept 30 days. Only refusals for contact details, requests for private information, severe language and rude language are counted, each kind on its own. We also count, per day, how many friends you typed to who never wrote back (the number only), and keep that count for the same 30 days.
  • Reports: A person only looks at a message when it is reported. A report keeps a copy of that message and up to five messages before it, which can include the reporter's own messages, for up to 90 days, or longer when the law requires it. A report made when you block or remove a friend, rather than about one message, keeps a copy of the last 20 messages of that chat instead, for the same time.
  • Automatic pauses: if someone reports you for sexual messages or for asking for personal details, typed chat may be paused automatically until we review it. Typed chat is also paused automatically:
    • for a while, after reports from several players;
    • for a day, after several of your messages in one day were refused for contact details, requests for private information or severe language;
    • for a day, if you type in one day to many friends (more than ten) who never wrote back;
    • for 10 minutes, after several refused messages in a short time.
    Your online profile keeps the state of any pause, hold or ban and how many automatic pauses it has had (see Reporting and blocking).
  • Removing a friend, blocking a player or deleting your data removes your chat with them from both screens at once. We keep a hidden copy for 14 days so that abuse can still be reported, then delete it.

You can turn typed chat off in Settings → Friends & chat; quick messages and stickers stay on.

  • Chat messages: up to 30 days, and at most the last 100 with each friend.
  • Which messages you have read: until you stop being friends.
  • Hidden copies of chats after a block, an unfriend or a deletion: 14 days.
  • Copies of reported messages: up to 90 days, or longer when the law requires it.
  • Counts of refused messages: 30 days.
  • The count of friends you typed to who never wrote back: 30 days.
  • Muted players: until you unmute them or stop being friends.
  • Reports about players (who reported whom, the reported display name, the reason, the match id and the time): with no fixed end date. Our server does not delete them by itself, because they are needed to moderate names and behaviour and to keep players safe; you can ask us to delete a report you made.
  • Our log of moderation actions (such as a chat pause or a ban, with the start of the player id and the display name): with no fixed end date.
  • The id of a deleted online profile (its player id, with the date, nothing else): one year after the deletion, so that an old backup can't bring the profile back; older ids are removed the next time a profile is deleted.

  • Your record on our support service (the one-way codes, support code, platform, app version, dates, how many times the game connected, gifts with their reasons and order numbers, and the details on the activity trail): until it is deleted as described in What a deletion removes, and what stays. The support service does not delete records that are no longer used, and Reset all data or uninstalling the app does not delete it.
  • What stays of a deleted record on our support service (an empty record, each gift's item, amount, state and dates, and the activity trail's event names and times, with nothing that identifies you): it has no end date.
  • A message waiting to reach our support service (the one-way codes of a sign-in, or the one-way code of a deletion): in a queue on our game server, until the support service has answered.
  • A gift on its way to your game: only in our game server's memory, while your game is connected.
  • Backups of our game server: deleted data can remain in its encrypted backups for up to 5 days.
  • Backups of our support service: deleted data can remain in them for up to about 30 days: for up to about 19 days on its encrypted disk at Fly.io, and for up to about 30 days in the encrypted copies at Cloudflare.
  • The support service's log: one line per request (time, path and result), with no id, code or address. Fly.io keeps it for 7 days. To limit requests, the service also counts them per player in its memory, for about an hour.
  • Server logs: the game server's running log notes events such as:
    • a new online profile (its player id and display name);
    • a room opening (with the name of the player who opened it);
    • a match starting and ending (with the players' display names, and whether the opponent was a computer player);
    • ranked results (with both player ids), reports and moderation actions;
    • once an hour, the timing and sticker totals described above.
    It notes them with pseudonymous player ids and without IP addresses, and it has no typed messages, gender or flag. It is used only to keep the server working and to handle reports. Fly.io keeps it only as long as its log service keeps logs; we do not export or archive it.
  • Support e-mails and ad reports, with the e-mail address they come from: only as long as we need them to deal with your message, and deleted within 12 months; you can ask us to delete yours sooner at any time.
  • Data held by the ad partners and by Apple or Google: for the periods set out in their own privacy policies.

Security

Everything the game sends to our server travels encrypted (over secure WebSockets), and our web pages are served over HTTPS. Our game server talks to our support service over HTTPS too.

  • On our server, the session key that keeps a device signed in is stored only as a scrambled code that can't be turned back into the key.
  • Your online profile is stored under your player id, not under the guest identifier itself. A signed-in account also keeps the guest identifier of the device it was linked to, to link the account to its profile.
  • IP addresses are never stored, and typed messages, gender and flag are never written to the logs.
  • Our game server, our support service and their data are hosted by Fly.io, on encrypted disks. Off-site copies of the support service's backups are encrypted before they leave it, with a key only we hold (see Who else handles data).

No system is completely secure, so we cannot promise that data will never be lost or exposed. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you, and the authorities, where the law requires it.

Children

Carrom Crew is intended for players aged 13 and older; it is not directed at children under 13. The game never asks for a real name, address, phone number or location. It asks for a year of birth only to tell whether you are under 13 (the year itself is not stored). The optional gender starts as Hidden. The flag is not a location: it starts as the flag of the device's region setting, and the player can change it or choose No flag (see Gender and flag).

If a player tells us they are under 13, the game limits what it collects and shows.

  • They can send and receive only quick messages and stickers (fixed texts and pictures, no typed messages).
  • Their ads are child-directed and never personalised. Before any ad loads, the game tells the ad partners that the ads are child-directed and that the player is under the age of consent. On Android the advertising ID permission is part of the app for every player.
  • The game does not ask them about tracking or personalised ads.
  • Their gender stays Hidden.
  • Their name shows as "Player" on public leaderboards. Other players still see the display name they chose in online matches, in friend lists and in friend invites, so it should never be their real name (our Terms ask every player not to put personal information in a name).
  • Sign in with Apple is not offered to them, so we receive no e-mail address and keep no cloud save for them. The one exception: a player who was already signed in with Apple before telling the game they are under 13 keeps that account, with its e-mail address and cloud save (Sign out and Delete account stay available); a parent can also ask us to delete it.
  • Computer players they may meet in Quick matches send only the built-in stickers; they never chat and cannot be added as friends.

So that they can play online and with friends, our server keeps only what the game needs for that, as described in this policy. That is their online profile (a player id, the display name they chose, character, optional flag, friend code, rating, star totals, medals and age group, and which version of the Terms they accepted and when), their friends and blocks, and the quick messages and stickers they exchange with friends (up to 30 days). We use this only to run the game and keep it safe, never for advertising, and we never contact them. Their online profile is kept until it is deleted: our server does not delete unused profiles by itself.

Like every player whose game goes online, they also get a support code and a record on our support service (see Your support code and gifts from our team). It holds no name and, apart from the one exception above, nothing made from an e-mail address. We use it only for support and gifts. A gift reaches them only as a fixed line in the game's Inbox. This record too is kept until it is deleted: the support service does not delete unused records by itself.

  • A parent or the player can delete it at any time with Settings → Privacy & data → Delete online data (which also clears their record on our support service and replaces the kept copy of their guest identifier; see Reinstalling, backups and your guest identifier), or by e-mailing us the friend code from the Friends screen.
  • A parent can also ask us to show them the data we hold about their child.
  • After Delete online data, the game sends nothing more to our server until the player goes online on purpose.

For everyone else we tell the ad partners that the game is not child-directed. If you believe we hold information about a child that we should not, contact us and we will delete it.

  • Delete account, in older versions: the game goes online again at once as a new guest, which starts a new online profile.
