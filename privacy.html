Carrom Crew — Privacy Policy

Updated 2 October 2026 · applies to Carrom Crew 1.1.0 and later

Who we are

Carrom Crew is made and published by Abdulla Alhashmi, an independent game developer ("we", "us"). We decide how the data described in this policy is used and are responsible for it. You can reach us at youtubeabdulla@gmail.com with any question about this policy, to ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted.

Summary

We do not sell your data for money, and the game has no analytics of its own. Carrom Crew shows ads delivered through Unity LevelPlay, which fills them from our ad partners Unity Ads and Google AdMob. These partners process some device data, such as your advertising identifier, to show and measure ads. They are asked to personalise ads only if you allow it (and, on iPhone and iPad, only if you also allow tracking). See "Advertising" and "Your ad choices". Your game progress is stored on your device. While the game is online it keeps a small online profile on our server for friends, leaderboards and online play, which includes your trick-shot star totals (see "Friends and leaderboards" and "Online matches"). Sign in with Apple is optional on iPhone and iPad; if you use it, a copy of your progress is kept on our server so it loads on your other devices (see "Optional Sign in with Apple and cloud save"). You can delete your online data in the app at any time (see "Deleting your data").

What the app stores on your device

  • Your progress: level, XP, trophies, owned items, Crew Pass progress, trick-shot stars, cup, Weekend League and weekly ladder progress, streaks and best times, settings, missions and achievements, and the results of your last 30 matches.
  • A random guest identifier (g-…) created on first launch. It contains no personal information.
  • The display name you choose (defaults to "Player").
  • If you sign in with Apple: the sign-in provider, the account id Apple gives Carrom Crew (the game uses it only to check with Apple that you still use Sign in with Apple for Carrom Crew), the e-mail address you chose to share and a session key that keeps this device signed in.
  • Your answer to the "Personalised ads" question, whether you own Remove Ads, and the counters that pace the ads (how many matches you have finished, when the last ad between matches was shown and how many reward videos you have watched today).

This lives in the app's private storage. To erase it, use Settings → Privacy & data → Reset all data or uninstall the app.

Optional Sign in with Apple and cloud save

On iPhone and iPad you can use Sign in with Apple from the sign-in page or from Settings → Account & name. Signing in is never required: guests can play everything. We never see or store your password. From the sign-in we keep: the sign-in provider (Apple), the account id Apple gives us for Carrom Crew, the e-mail address you choose to share (Apple lets you hide it; we then receive Apple's private relay address instead), the random guest identifier of the device you signed in on, which links the account to your online profile, a session key (stored only as a one-way hash) that keeps that device signed in, and the token Apple gives our server for your sign-in (a "refresh token"), which we use only to ask Apple to revoke Carrom Crew's Sign in with Apple access when you delete your account. We use the rest only to recognise your account. While you are signed in, the app uploads a copy of your game progress to our server and loads it when you sign in on another device: your display name, level, XP, trophies, owned items, Crew Pass, missions, achievements, trick-shot stars, cup, Weekend League and weekly ladder progress, streaks and best times, and settings, your 20 most recent match results (including matches against the computer: result, opponent's name and mode, XP and time), your 20 latest inbox messages, the random guest identifier, your ad choice, whether you own Remove Ads, and the ad counters. Signing out keeps your progress on the device and stops syncing. We never pass your account, e-mail address or player id to the ad partners.

Deleting your data

  • Signed in with Apple: use Settings → Account & name → Delete account at any time; the game asks you to confirm once with Apple. This immediately removes from our server the sign-in link, your cloud save, your e-mail address, your online profile, rating, friends, blocks, ranked match results and leaderboard entries. Our server also asks Apple to revoke Carrom Crew's Sign in with Apple access, which removes Carrom Crew from your Apple Account, and deletes the Apple refresh token with your account. If Apple can't complete that right away, our server keeps that token for this one purpose and tries again for up to 7 days, together with a one-way scrambled form (a keyed hash) of your Apple user id, so that the retry is dropped if you sign in with the same Apple ID again. If Carrom Crew still appears in your Apple Account afterwards, you can remove it yourself: open the iPhone or iPad Settings app, tap your name, then Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple, choose Carrom Crew and stop using Sign in with Apple for it.
  • Playing as a guest: use Settings → Privacy & data → Delete online data at any time. This immediately removes from our server your online profile (display name, character, friend code, rating and trick-shot star totals), your friends and friend requests, blocks, ranked match results and leaderboard entries. Your progress on the device stays. The game then stays offline until you go online on purpose (by opening Friends or Leaderboards, playing online or signing in), which starts a new profile with a new friend code. You can also e-mail youtubeabdulla@gmail.com with the friend code shown on the Friends screen, and we delete the same data within one month (usually much sooner) and confirm by e-mail.
  • On your device: Settings → Privacy & data → Reset all data, or uninstall the app.

Reports you made, and reports other players made about you, are kept for moderation (see "How long data is kept"). Data held by the ad partners is not stored by us; see "Your ad choices" for how to ask them.

Playing against the computer

Matches against computer opponents, trick-shot puzzles and the lessons are played on your device, and the moves you make in them are never sent anywhere. Some results do leave the device: your trick-shot star totals go to our server for the leaderboards (see "Friends and leaderboards"), a copy of your progress and recent match results is uploaded if you are signed in (see "Optional Sign in with Apple and cloud save"), and when an ad is shown after a match the ad partners receive the data described in "Advertising". Ads are never shown during a match or during the lessons.

Online matches

If you play online (a quick or ranked match, or a room with a friend), the app connects to the Carrom Crew match server to pair you with another player. During the match the server receives your display name, your chosen character, a temporary session token and your moves (striker position, aim and the resulting board), and relays them to your opponent, who sees your display name and character. Your moves are kept in memory only while the match is running and are not stored. The result of a ranked match (winner, loser, how the match ended, rating change and time) is stored with your online profile so ratings and leaderboards work; quick matches and friend rooms are not recorded. The server does not store your IP address beyond the open connection. There is no chat — only the built-in emote stickers can be sent.

Friends and leaderboards

A few seconds after you finish the first-launch screens, and whenever the game runs with an internet connection, it connects to the Carrom Crew server by itself, even if you never play online (after you use Delete online data it waits until you go online on purpose; see "Deleting your data"). The server then keeps an online profile for your device: a pseudonymous player id (a keyed hash of the random guest id created on your device — not a device identifier, your name or your e-mail), your display name, your chosen character, your friend code, when the profile was created and last seen, your rating and online wins and losses, your trick-shot star totals (all-time and this week, which the game sends by itself whenever it connects, after each match and when you open the leaderboards) and your friends list. Other players can see your display name, character, rating and star totals on the leaderboards, including the public Trick shots and This week boards, and your friends can see whether you are online. Friend invites carry your name and a private room code; they are held only in the server's memory, not stored, and disappear from your friend's screen after a minute. The rating of online matches is worked out by the server from the match result. To have this profile deleted, see "Deleting your data".

Reporting and blocking

You can report another player's name or behaviour and block players from the Leaderboards, Friends and online result screens. A report stores your pseudonymous player id, the reported player's display name (and their player id if they have an online profile), the reason, the match id if any and the time, so we can review it. Blocking stores the pair of player ids. Names reported by several players are hidden, and offensive names are refused.

Advertising

Carrom Crew shows two kinds of ads:

  • Rewarded videos — only when you tap to watch one for the reward named on the button (for example bonus XP in the Shop). Closing one early simply gives no reward.
  • Short ads between matches — one is shown on the way out of a match that you finish, against the computer or online, or after a solved trick shot, from your first match on and at least a minute apart. They are never shown during a match, during the lessons, after a forfeited match, or once you own Remove Ads.

The ads are delivered by Unity LevelPlay, the ad mediation service of Unity Technologies (formerly ironSource). For each ad, LevelPlay picks an ad from one of our ad partners: Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) or Google AdMob (Google). Their software in the app collects the data below and sends it to them; we do not receive it. They use it under their own privacy policies:

  • Advertising identifier and other device identifiers: on Android, the Google advertising ID (unless you have deleted it in Android settings) and the app set ID; on iPhone and iPad, Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, and the identifier for vendor (IDFV).
  • Device information: device model and maker, operating system and version, language, time zone, screen size, network type and mobile carrier, and the app's name and version.
  • Ad interactions: which ads were requested, shown, watched to the end, skipped or tapped, and whether an advertised app was later installed.
  • Approximate location: estimated from your IP address (roughly country or city). The game never uses GPS and never asks for location permission.
  • Diagnostic data about the ad software, such as load times, errors and crashes.

The ad partners use this data to deliver ads, to choose which ads to show, to limit how often you see the same ad, to measure how ads perform (which is how we are paid), and to detect fraud and invalid traffic. If you have not allowed personalised ads, we tell them so and they are asked to show only non-personalised ads, chosen from context such as the game and your approximate location rather than from a profile of you; they still process the data above to deliver, limit, measure and protect the ads. Their policies: Unity (Unity LevelPlay, ironSource and Unity Ads), Google and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Ads need an internet connection. If an ad cannot be loaded, you simply keep playing. We do not choose the individual ads that the partners show; if you see an ad that seems inappropriate, please report it (see "Reporting an ad").

Who else handles data

  • The ad partners above (Unity and Google), through their software in the app, for the ad purposes described in "Advertising".
  • Fly.io, which hosts our game server (in Singapore) and stores the server data described in this policy for us. It may not use that data for its own purposes.
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases) and Google (Google Play purchases), under their own privacy policies.

We share data only with these companies, only for the purposes described here, and we never sell it. Each of them is bound by its own privacy policy, its agreement with us and the App Store and Google Play rules to protect that data at least as well as this policy describes.

Your ad choices

  • Personalised ads. Once, the first time you open the game (right after the Start screen), it asks whether ads may be personalised: No thanks or Yes, personalise. The game plays the same whatever you answer, you see the same number of ads, and neither answer earns a reward; with No thanks you still see ads, but they are not personalised. You can change your answer, and so withdraw your consent, at any time with the Personalised ads switch in Settings; it applies to the ads loaded after the change.
  • Tracking on iPhone and iPad. After that question, iOS asks once whether Carrom Crew may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the ad partners cannot use Apple's advertising identifier. You can change it at any time in the iPhone or iPad Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID. You can delete or reset it in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Remove Ads. A one-time purchase in the Shop (also offered in Settings) that turns off the ads between matches for good. Rewarded videos stay available, but they only ever play when you choose to watch one. The game re-applies it automatically when it starts, once the App Store or Google Play has confirmed your purchases; after a reinstall or on a new device, Settings → Restore purchases brings it back. It is also part of your cloud save when you are signed in with Apple.
  • Data the ad partners hold. We cannot see or delete it. To access or delete it, contact Unity or Google through the policies linked above; they recognise you by your advertising identifier.
  • US state privacy laws. Some US state laws call giving ad partners your data for personalised ads a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. When you answer No thanks, or turn the Personalised ads switch off, the game sends the ad partners (through Unity LevelPlay) the US "do not sell or share my personal information" opt-out signal together with the European "no consent to personalised ads" signal. You still see ads, but they are not personalised. When you answer Yes, personalise, or turn the switch on, the game sends consent and no opt-out instead. Your current choice is sent at every launch before any ad loads, and again whenever you change the switch.
  • Report an ad. Settings → Report an ad, or e-mail us; see "Reporting an ad" below.

Reporting an ad

If you see an ad that seems inappropriate, misleading or broken, use Settings → Report an ad (in the Ads card, right below Personalised ads; Settings → Contact support and Settings → Privacy & data have the same button). It opens your e-mail app with a report to youtubeabdulla@gmail.com that already contains the details of the last ad shown since you opened the game (reward video or ad between matches, the ad network, the ad's creative id, the ad unit and the time), the app version and your device's operating system; if no ad has been shown since you opened the game, the report says so. Add what you saw (a screenshot helps) and send it; nothing is sent until you send the e-mail yourself. You can also simply e-mail that address with the game's name, the time and, if you can, a screenshot. We use a report, and the e-mail address it comes from, only to look into the ad (for example to have it blocked through the ad partner) and to reply to you.

Purchases

Carrom Crew sells optional cosmetic items (strikers, boards and effects), character packs, the Everything Bundle, the Crew Pass and Remove Ads through the Apple App Store and Google Play billing. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google Play; the app only receives a confirmation that the purchase succeeded, and we never see your payment details. Purchases stay with your App Store or Google Play account. Each time the game starts it re-applies the items you own, such as Remove Ads, once the store has answered; after a reinstall or on a new device they are brought back with Settings → Restore purchases.

How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you use Reset all data or uninstall the app.
  • Online profile (name, character, friend code, rating, star totals, friends, blocks and ranked match results): for as long as the profile exists, until it is deleted as described in "Deleting your data".
  • Sign in with Apple link, e-mail address, Apple refresh token and cloud save: until you use Delete account. If Apple can't complete the revocation right away, the refresh token and a keyed hash of your Apple user id are kept only to ask Apple again (and to cancel that retry if you sign in with the same Apple ID again), for up to 7 days.
  • Moves of an online match: only in the server's memory while the match is running.
  • Server logs: the game server's running log notes events such as a room opening, a match starting and ending (with the players' display names) and reports, with pseudonymous player ids and without IP addresses. It is used only to keep the server working and to handle reports. Fly.io keeps it only as long as its log service keeps logs; we do not export or archive it.
  • Reports about players: for as long as they are needed to moderate names and behaviour and to keep players safe; you can ask us to delete a report you made.
  • Support e-mails and ad reports, with the e-mail address they come from: only as long as we need them to deal with your message, and deleted within 12 months; you can ask us to delete yours sooner at any time.
  • Data held by the ad partners and by Apple or Google: for the periods set out in their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time for a copy of the data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it or to stop using it, and you can withdraw a consent you gave (for example with the Personalised ads switch) without affecting what was done before. E-mail youtubeabdulla@gmail.com; for an online profile, include the friend code from the Friends screen so we can find it. We answer within one month. Depending on where you live, you may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Children

Carrom Crew's content is suitable for all ages (App Store 4+, Everyone / PEGI 3), and the game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We tell the ad partners that the game is not child-directed. The game never asks for a real name, age, address, phone number or location, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information (for example an e-mail address through Sign in with Apple), contact us and we will delete it.

Permissions

The app uses network access (for online play, friends and leaderboards, the optional cloud save, ads and store purchases), the store's billing service for purchases. It does not use the device's vibration. The Android version declares the advertising ID permission used by the ad partners, and on iPhone and iPad the game asks once for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). It does not access the camera, microphone, contacts, device location or photo library.

Changes to this policy

When the app changes how it handles data, we update this page before that version is released; the date at the top shows the latest change.
