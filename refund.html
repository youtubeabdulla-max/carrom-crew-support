Carrom Crew — Refund Policy

Updated 31 October 2026 · applies to Carrom Crew 1.0.0 and later

This page describes the current version of the app, 1.3.0.

Also read our Terms of Service (this policy is part of them) and Privacy Policy, or go to the support page.

This policy explains how refunds work for purchases in Carrom Crew, a game made and published by Abdulla Alhashmi ("we", "us").

In short

  • We don't give refunds ourselves. Apple or Google decide refunds under their own rules.
  • The law where you live may also give you a right to a refund.
  • Something you bought didn't arrive? Use Settings → Restore purchases, then e-mail us.

What you can buy

Carrom Crew is free to play. It sells optional cosmetic items (strikers, disc sets and effects), character packs (in the Shop's Bundles tab), Elite, Phantom and Zenith characters (each with its own set of items), and Remove Ads, which turns off the ads between matches. Each is a one-time purchase at the price the store shows in the game before you buy. There are no subscriptions, no in-game currency and nothing random, and nothing you buy changes how a match plays.

Who takes your payment

Every purchase is made through the Apple App Store (on iPhone and iPad) or Google Play (on Android), with the payment method of your store account. Apple or Google takes the payment. The game receives the store's confirmation and receipt, which it reads on your device and never sends to us. We never see your payment details.

Asking for a refund

Because Apple and Google take the payment, they also decide refunds, under their own refund policies. The law where you live may also give you a right to a refund.

  • App Store (iPhone and iPad): ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Only Apple can refund an App Store purchase.
  • Google Play (Android): see Google Play's refund help page, Google Play refund policies, which explains how and when Google Play gives refunds and how to ask for one.
  • An item that does not work, or is not as described: tell us (see A purchase that did not arrive for what to send). Under consumer law you may have a right to a repair, a replacement or a refund.

When your item arrives

An item you buy is added to your game as soon as the store confirms the payment, usually straight away, so you can use it at once. A purchase that is waiting for approval (for example a parent's approval) or for the payment to go through is added as soon as the store confirms it; you can keep playing in the meantime.

A purchase that did not arrive

  1. Make sure you are online and signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with, then use Settings → Restore purchases in the game. It works with or without a Carrom Crew account.
  2. If the store says the purchase is waiting for approval or for the payment, it arrives once the store confirms it.
  3. If it is still missing, e-mail us at youtubeabdulla@gmail.com with the order number from the store's receipt e-mail or from your purchase history in the store, the item, and roughly when you bought it. Settings → Contact support → Email support opens an e-mail to us that already names the app version and, once the game has one, your Player ID. We answer within 2 working days.

Restoring purchases

Purchases stay with the App Store or Google Play account that made them. Each time the game starts, it re-applies the items you own, such as Remove Ads, once the store has answered. After a reinstall or on a new device, use Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to that store account. App Store purchases can be restored only on iPhone and iPad, and Google Play purchases only on Android.

Items that are no longer sold:

  • The Everything Bundle is no longer sold. If you bought it, Restore purchases still brings back everything in it that is still in the game.
  • Board skins, sold up to version 1.1.0, were removed from the game for every player in version 1.2.0, so they no longer show. This includes the board skins in the Everything Bundle and in character packs bought before then, and the board items that were once sold on their own. Board skins are an exception to our promise not to take away an item you paid for (section 4 of our Terms of Service). If you bought a board on its own, you can ask Apple or Google for a refund as described in Asking for a refund.
  • The season pass, sold until version 1.1.0, is no longer sold either. If you bought it, Restore purchases gives you its twelve Legendary items.

Purchases by children

If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before you buy anything (see "Who may play" in our Terms of Service). Parents can make the store ask for their approval or password before each purchase, in the App Store's or Google Play's own settings. If a purchase was made without permission, ask Apple or Google for a refund as described above.

Your rights

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under the law that applies to you.
