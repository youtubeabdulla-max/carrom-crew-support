Support

Need help with Carrom Crew or Chromo Rush, want to report a player, or want your online profile deleted? E-mail youtubeabdulla@gmail.com. For a Carrom Crew profile, include the friend code shown on the Friends screen. We answer within 2 working days and act on abuse reports within 24 hours.

Signed-in Carrom Crew players can delete their account in the app: Settings → Account & name → Delete account. You confirm once with Apple, and this also ends Sign in with Apple for Carrom Crew. If Carrom Crew still shows in the iPhone or iPad Settings app → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple afterwards, you can remove it there. Carrom Crew players who are not signed in delete their online profile in the app with Settings → Privacy & data → Delete online data, or e-mail us the friend code from the Friends screen and we delete it. The page Delete your Carrom Crew account and data explains every way, also without the app. For Chromo Rush, see its own support page.

Both games are made by Abdulla Alhashmi.

Ads in Carrom Crew

Carrom Crew shows ads delivered through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies, formerly ironSource) from our ad partners Unity Ads, ironSource Ads and Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for the reward named on the button, and a short ad after a match you finish or a trick shot you solve (at most one a minute). Those short ads never appear during a match, during the lessons, after a forfeited match, or once you own Remove Ads, and a rewarded video only plays when you tap to watch one. If an ad does not load, just carry on playing; there is nothing to fix.

  • Personalised ads: change your answer at any time with the Personalised ads switch in Settings. Either answer shows the same number of ads. With No thanks (switch off) the game sends the ad partners both the US "do not sell or share" opt-out signal and the European "no consent" signal, so the ads you see are not personalised.
  • Tracking on iPhone and iPad: iPhone or iPad Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: delete or reset it in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Remove Ads: a one-time purchase in the Shop that turns off the ads between matches. The game re-applies it automatically when it starts; after a reinstall or on a new phone, use Settings → Restore purchases.
  • Report an ad: Settings → Report an ad (right below Personalised ads) opens your e-mail app with a report to us that already contains the ad network, the ad's creative id and the time of the last ad shown since you opened the game. Add what you saw and send it. You can also simply e-mail us with the game name, the time and, if you can, a screenshot.
  • A rewarded video gave no reward? E-mail us with the game name and the time.

Carrom Crew: who you play online

Who will I play online? Other players. When few players are online, a Quick match may be against a computer player instead of a long wait. Ranked matches and rooms with a friend never use computer players. Computer players never change ratings or the leaderboards. They play on the same board with the same physics, send stickers only and can't be added as friends. Their strength follows your own (your Ranked rating, or your trophies if you have not played Ranked), never what you buy or the ads you watch. The game says this in the online lobby, while it looks for a Quick match and in Settings → Help. To always play the computer, choose vs Computer on Home.

Was my opponent a computer player? If it was a Quick match while few players were online, it may have been: the game does not label players in a match. A computer player's name, character, disc set, flag and medal are picked for that one match; they are not anyone's account. E-mail us the time and your opponent's name, and we will tell you if we can still check that match.
